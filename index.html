Value Guide

Understand what the values in Match Attax Vault mean before buying, selling or trading.

💰 Estimated Value

Values are collector-market estimates rather than official retail prices. Market prices can move as demand and availability change.

✨ Card Rarity

Base cards, Limited Editions, Black Edge, Infinity, Hall of Fame and other chase cards can have very different market values.

🛡️ Condition Matters

The Vault generally uses Near Mint as its valuation reference. Damage, bends, scratches or poor corners can reduce a card's actual selling price.

📈 Demand Matters

Popular players and scarce cards can trade above normal category averages when collector demand rises.

🌍 Market Differences

International sale prices and Indian collector-market prices may differ. Vault values are presented in INR for easier local comparison.

⚖️ Trading

Value is only one part of a trade. Personal preference, scarcity and collecting goals may also influence what feels fair to individual collectors.

About Match Attax Vault

A collector-focused tool for understanding cards and making smarter trades.

Match Attax Vault is an independent collector database and valuation tool built to make Match Attax collecting easier to understand.

The Vault connects card identification, estimated values and trade comparison in one place. Each card is tracked using a unique Card ID so that different versions of the same player can be distinguished correctly.

Trade fairness system

🟢 FAIR — value difference of 10% or less
🟡 SLIGHTLY UNEVEN — difference above 10% and up to 25%
🔴 UNFAIR BY VALUE — difference above 25%

Match Attax Vault is an independent collector project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official service of Topps. Card values are estimates and actual transaction prices may vary.
